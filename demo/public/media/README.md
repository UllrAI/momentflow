# Experience photography

`zipline.jpg`, `kart.jpg` and `forest.jpg` were generated with built-in imagegen on 2026-10-24. People are fictional adults, not real identified visitors. The first two images are used as raw inputs to the real recognition pipeline; the forest photo exercises the no-face branch.

Prompts: two fictional adult East Asian friends with white helmets and full harnesses at a pine-forest zipline platform; two fictional adult East Asian friends safely driving mountain go-karts; an unoccupied pine valley with distant mountains. Warm natural light, professional photography, no UI, text, brands or watermark.

Pre-made videos and legacy runtime logo crops have been removed. Personal highlights are now generated by the backend from the selected raw inputs, not replaced by a fixed sample video.

See `../samples/README.md` for kindergarten assets, actual motion test footage, model attribution and synthetic fixture boundaries.
